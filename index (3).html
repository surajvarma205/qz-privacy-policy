Qz Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

01 Introduction

Qz is a quiz and knowledge application designed for learning and entertainment. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Qz.

02 Local Data Storage

Qz stores gameplay information such as XP, virtual coins, level progress, streaks, achievements, and app preferences locally on the user's device.

03 Personal Information

Qz does not require users to create an account. Qz does not directly request names, passwords, email addresses, or other directly identifying information from users.

04 Google AdMob Advertising

Qz uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob and related advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising interaction information, and other information required to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, subject to Google's policies and applicable laws.

05 Third-Party Services

Qz uses Google AdMob for advertising.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

06 Data Processing

Information processed by third-party advertising services may be handled according to their own privacy policies and terms.

07 Data Security

Qz is designed so that gameplay progress is stored locally on the user's device. Qz does not require a user account database for normal gameplay.

08 Children's Privacy

Qz is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to play.

09 Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Qz features, services, or data practices change. The updated version will display a new "Last updated" date.
